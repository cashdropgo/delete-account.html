Account & Data Deletion

How to Delete Your CashDrop GO Account

Option 1: In-App Deletion

  • Open CashDrop GO app
  • Go to Profile tab
  • Tap "Delete Account"
  • Enter your password to confirm
  • Your account will be permanently deleted

Option 2: Email Request

  • Send an email to cashdropyono@gmail.com
  • Subject: "Account Deletion Request"
  • Include your registered email address
  • Your account will be deleted within 7 business days

What happens when you delete your account?

  • Your profile and personal information will be permanently deleted
  • Your wallet balance will be forfeited (please withdraw funds first)
  • Your dropped banknotes will be anonymized
  • Your forum posts will be anonymized
  • This action is irreversible

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account credentials (email, password)
  • Display name and profile picture
  • Transaction history
  • Login sessions
  • Daily challenges progress
  • Achievement data

Data That Will Be Anonymized

  • Dropped banknotes (author changed to "Deleted User")
  • Forum posts and comments (author changed to "Deleted User")
